You do not need to be a developer to secure your small business website. This checklist covers the most important security checks you can carry out — and most of them take less than 5 minutes to verify.

1. SSL certificate is valid

Every website should use HTTPS. Check that your SSL certificate is valid, issued by a trusted authority, and covers your exact domain. Use our SSL Checker or look for the padlock icon in your browser.

2. Certificate renewal is automatic

An expired SSL certificate shows a security warning to every visitor. Make sure your hosting provider renews certificates automatically, or set a calendar reminder 45 days before your certificate expiry date.

3. SPF record is configured

An SPF record in your DNS tells the world which mail servers are authorised to send email on your behalf. Without one, your emails are more likely to land in spam — and criminals can spoof your address.

4. DMARC policy is set

A DMARC policy tells receiving mail servers what to do when an email fails authentication. Start with p=none to monitor, then move to p=reject to block fraudulent emails completely.

5. Security headers are present

Security headers are HTTP response headers that tell browsers how to behave on your website. Key headers include Content-Security-Policy, X-Frame-Options, and Strict-Transport-Security.

6. DNS records are correct

Incorrect DNS records can make your website or email unreachable. Verify that your A, MX, and CNAME records are correctly configured and not pointing to old or obsolete services.

7. Monitoring is active

All the checks in this list mean nothing if a problem develops between checks. Set up automated monitoring so you are alerted the moment anything changes.
