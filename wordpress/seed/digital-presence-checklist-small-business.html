Your digital presence is everything a potential customer can find about you online — your website, your Google Business Profile, your social media accounts, and your online reviews. Monitoring and maintaining all of these is increasingly essential for any small business.

Your website

Your website is your digital storefront. At minimum, it should be always available (uptime monitoring), fast (performance monitoring), secure (SSL, DNS, email security, security headers), and up to date.

Your domain

Your domain name is one of your most important business assets. Make sure it is registered in your name, renews automatically, and that your contact details with your registrar are current. A lapsed domain can be immediately registered by someone else.

Your email

If you send email from your domain (info@yourbusiness.com), you need SPF, DKIM and DMARC records. Without them, your emails land in spam folders and your domain can be spoofed.

Your online reviews

Reviews on Google, Tripadvisor, and other platforms are powerful trust signals. A negative review left unanswered looks worse than no response at all. Set up review monitoring so you can respond promptly.

Your Google Business Profile

Verify and maintain your Google Business Profile. Accurate opening hours, contact details, and regular photo updates improve local search visibility and customer trust.
